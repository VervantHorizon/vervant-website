Privacy on this information site

Thanks for visiting Vervant. This site shares product information, guides and a browser-only invoice preview. It does not currently offer account registration, hosted PDF generation, payment collection, advertising pixels or first-party analytics.

Information you choose to share

Some links open GitHub Discussions or repositories. Those services are operated by GitHub and handle information under their own terms and privacy notice. To keep your information safe, please don’t post passwords, API keys, customer documents or anything confidential in a public discussion.

PDF documents and accounts

The hosted Vervant API is not open yet, and this site does not accept customer documents. Please don’t send personal, confidential or production data to the project. Before account registration opens, we’ll publish a separate privacy notice and explain how documents are stored, retained and deleted.
