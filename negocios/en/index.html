BEFORE YOU START

Questions buyers usually ask.

How do I pay for the company sheet?

In two equal parts. With the catalog, the US$258 total is paid as US$129 to begin and US$129 after you approve the preview, before final delivery. For the standalone sheet, the total is US$99: two payments of US$49.50.

What exactly do I receive?

A one-page PDF in Letter or A4 size, with a short company introduction, up to 6 services or capabilities, industries, materials, processes, up to 3 photos and contact links. One revision round is included.

Do I need design or computer skills?

No. You can send your business details, notes, logo and photos as email attachments from your phone. Vervant organizes the information, prepares a preview and applies your revision before delivering the PDF.

What is the difference between the sheet and the catalog?

The sheet introduces your company on one page so a buyer can understand your capabilities. The catalog lets buyers browse up to 12 products or services and prepare an enquiry with project details.

Do you work with companies outside your country?

Yes. Vervant Local is delivered remotely. The standard sales and delivery language is English, with prices quoted in US dollars.

How do I share the finished catalog?

You receive a self-contained HTML file and a short publishing guide for your current website. If you do not have hosting yet, we can outline simple options and confirm any setup cost before the project begins.

What kind of enquiries does it support?

Buyers can share dimensions, materials, quantities, location, target date and project details. The message goes to the business WhatsApp number or email address you choose.

How is payment arranged?

In two equal installments: US$99.50 to start after you approve the written proposal, and US$99.50 after you review and approve the preview, before final delivery.

Can you match our brand?

Yes. Choose from 12 visual directions and personalize your colors, logo, cover image, typography, image framing and product presentation.

Can we request changes?

One revision round is included after you review the preview. We confirm the scope and delivery schedule in writing before work begins.

How does the catalog help the business?

It organizes your offering and helps interested buyers explain what they need before requesting a quote. Results also depend on where you share the link and how you follow up.

Tell us what your business needs to quote.

Your written proposal is free and carries no commitment. We confirm price, scope and schedule before you make the first payment.
