BEFORE YOU START

Questions buyers usually ask.

Do you work with companies outside your country?

Yes. Vervant Local is delivered remotely. The current sales and delivery language is English; projects are quoted in US dollars unless another currency is agreed in writing.

Do we need WhatsApp?

No. The catalog can use a business email address instead, or offer both channels where appropriate.

Do you host the catalog?

The standard handover is a self-contained HTML file. You can publish it through your current hosting provider. Domain and paid hosting are not included.

Does it accept orders or payments?

No. It helps a buyer prepare an enquiry. Your team reviews the details and issues the actual quotation and payment terms.

Does Vervant store incoming enquiries?

No. The catalogue opens the visitor’s email or WhatsApp app with a prepared message. The visitor chooses whether to send it to your business. Vervant does not operate a lead database or automatically receive/store those enquiries.

Will it guarantee more leads or sales?

No. Results depend on your offer, audience, distribution and follow-up. The purpose is to make product information clearer and incoming enquiries more useful.

Can you use our photos and logo?

Only after you confirm that you own the materials or have permission to use them. Sample catalogs use fictional names and details.

Tell us what your business needs to quote.

We will review the fit, confirm the deliverables and price in writing, and request approved content only after the scope is agreed.
