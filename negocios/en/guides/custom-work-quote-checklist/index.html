COPYABLE MESSAGE

Prepare a request the workshop can review.

Complete what you know and mark anything that still needs discussion as “to be confirmed”. Then copy the message and paste it into the supplier’s email or messaging channel.

Tip: attach photos or drawings only when you are authorized to share them. Avoid sending confidential documents that are not needed to prepare a quote.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote.

Do I need every measurement?

No. Send the measurements you have, a photo or a sketch, and explain what is still unknown. The supplier can tell you what else is needed.

What if I have not chosen the material?

Describe the intended use, the finish you like and the project conditions. You can ask for options, but make it clear the material is still undecided.

Should I attach a drawing?

If you have one and are authorized to share it, it can help. If not, a visual reference and initial measurements are useful starting points.

Why include location and timing?

Delivery, installation, site access and the requested deadline can affect planning and the scope of work.
