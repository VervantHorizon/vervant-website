02 · BY INDUSTRY

The useful details depend on the job.

Use a checklist that matches what you need made or installed.

WOOD AND FURNITURE

Custom furniture

• Type of item
• Width × height × depth
• Wood, board and finish
• Installation and delivery

VISUAL COMMUNICATIONS

Signage and print

• Type of sign or print
• Dimensions and substrate
• Lighting and artwork
• Location and mounting

INDUSTRY AND FABRICATION

Metal and CNC

• Process and material
• Thickness and dimensions
• Drawing, tolerances and quantity
• Finish and target date

WHEN SOMETHING IS MISSING

Mark it as undecided

A photo, sketch or initial reference can help the workshop guide your next step.

Do not guess measurements or specifications. Share what you know and ask about the rest.
